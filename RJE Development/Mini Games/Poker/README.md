# Poker

Play at the open table or against the house. The archive also includes the original observer-poker prototype.

[799X](Standalone/799x/) · [Solo Foots](Standalone/solo/) · [FFA Foots](Standalone/ffa/)

799X has separate [open-table](Standalone/799x/open-table.j) and [house-table](Standalone/799x/house-table.j) code. Solo Foots supports a second player or practice against the computer. FFA Foots has a house opponent. The [observer-poker prototype](../Shared%20Casino/Development%20history/observer-poker/) includes its build scripts, artwork tools and rule tests. Poker and blackjack share the [card and chip textures](https://github.com/NorthStarEnergy/Footmen-Frenzy/tree/d6dcd92745124982f2082aa0aaa45c31592624cc/RJE%20Development/Mini%20Games/Shared%20Casino/Assets/PokerUI/).
